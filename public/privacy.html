Open TaskPal

Your tasks stay in this browser.

TaskPal’s task-saving feature uses storage in your browser profile. The app does not send your task titles or completion states to a task server. It has no account-based synchronization or cloud task backup.

Local storage is not a private vault

Someone using the same browser profile may be able to see your tasks. Browser extensions and scripts running with access to this site may also access local data. TaskPal does not provide an encrypted vault. Do not treat the task list as a place to store passwords or other secrets.

Retention and backups

Clearing this site’s browser data removes its saved tasks. Private browsing, browser cleanup and storage limits can affect how long data remains. Deleting a task updates the saved list when saving succeeds. Exported backup files stay on your device until you remove them, and they contain your task text.

Fonts and hosting

This site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google’s font services, which receive the network information needed to serve those files. Task titles are not included in the font URLs. The site is hosted on Vercel; ordinary site requests are handled by that hosting service.

Analytics

This version of the application does not include a visitor-analytics SDK, advertising pixel or session-replay script. No task-content tracking has been added. Hosting infrastructure may still maintain operational request logs; this page does not make claims about settings outside the application.

Your choices

Use a browser profile you trust, export backups for important tasks, and clear this site’s data when you want to remove its local list. Use Backup & storage on the task page to download or import a task backup.
